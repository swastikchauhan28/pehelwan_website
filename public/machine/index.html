Pehelwan / the machine

A tractor designed around the row.

The current Pehelwan build puts electric drive into a narrow chassis intended for work where conventional machinery needs more room.

Machine innovation

Every turn matters.

The team is developing the parts of a tractor that make space, control and attachments work together.

Steering

Quadra steer

Four-wheel steering is designed to help manoeuvre in tight spaces. The team describes this as a first for Indian agriculture; independent industry verification is pending.

Drive

Multi-speed gearbox

A gearbox designed to provide different working speeds for different tasks.

Implements

Custom three-point linkage

A purpose-built connection for the attachments the team is developing.

Control

Remote or steering

The design includes operation by remote and by steering wheel. Field use and availability remain subject to testing.
