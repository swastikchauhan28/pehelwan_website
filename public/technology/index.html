Vision AI roadmap

Intelligence begins
with observation.

From visual information to a path and a precise action: three connected ideas under development, each requiring field validation.

01 / ObserveIn development

A closer look at each plant

Research into recognising crops and weeds, with visual observations that may help describe plant health.

02 / NavigateIn development

Find the way through

Research into using crop-row information to plan how the machine moves through a field.

03 / TreatPlanned

Work at the right spot

Targeted micro spray is planned for detected weeds. Actual capability depends on field validation.

04 / GPS sowing · In development

Position informs the work.

The team aims to connect location information with the sowing implement. Accuracy, supported crops and operating conditions depend on field validation.
