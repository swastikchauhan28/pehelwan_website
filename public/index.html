MAMA’s drone spraying work brought the team into conversations with farmers. Timely work and service close to home kept coming up. Those conversations became the starting point for Pehelwan.

Between the rows. Around the turns.

Crop spacing and turning room change what a machine needs to do. Pehelwan’s narrow chassis and four-wheel steering design respond to that everyday reality.
