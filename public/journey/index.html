Pehelwan / our journey

Built from conversations in the field.

Before the tractor, the team worked with farmers through agricultural drones and spraying. What they heard shaped the next machine.

How the work progressed

One step led to the next.

2023

Drone spraying began

MAMA started agricultural drone work and built a service relationship with growers. Timely work and support close to home came up again and again.

April 2024

Ten days on the road

A trip from Pune to Yavatmal took the team into farmers’ fields to hear where equipment fell short.

2026

Pehelwan took shape

The team applied those lessons to a compact electric ground machine, with design work focused on narrow crop rows, steering and useful implements.

Farming roots

Problems close to home.

Founder Dheeraj Borde grew up in a farming family. The company’s work began with problems he knew firsthand and has continued through regular contact with people working in the field.

Pehelwan is one result of listening, building and returning to test the ideas.
